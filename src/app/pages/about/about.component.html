Presentation of Our Solution

We are proposing a machine-learning–powered NOVA classification prediction service that estimates the processing level of food products/items even when critical information is missing. This is already the case in Open Food Facts where some items are missing their NOVA classifications.

What it will do:

  • Automatically predict an item NOVA score (1 to 4)
  • Work before full data collection is completed
  • Help users, educators, and researchers evaluate food processing levels instantly
  • Integrate with the Open Food Facts databases

Our solution can be used as:

  • An API
  • An embedded component inside health or nutrition apps

We are also planning a simple website to share our results and explain how predictions are made.

 Technological Aspect

Technological Components:

  • Machine Learning Model
  • Gradient Boosting Ensemble (XGBoost / LightGBM / CatBoost)

Why this choice? Because it:

  • Handles missing values natively
  • Performs well with heterogeneous structured data
  • Achieves high accuracy without extensive preprocessing

Key attributes from Open Food Facts used for prediction:

  • ingredients_text, ingredients_tags, ingredients_analysis_tags, additives_n, additives_tags
  • categories, pnns_groups_1, pnns_groups_2, food_groups, food_groups_tags
  • main_category, main_category_en, product_name, generic_name, packaging
  • brands, labels, allergens, traces
