How It Works

1
Gather Product Information

We collect details from food products such as ingredients, categories, packaging, and labels. Even with some missing data, our model can still make an accurate estimate.

2
Machine Learning Analysis

The predictor uses Gradient Boosting, a type of machine learning that identifies patterns in the data to estimate the degree of processing.

3
Get Your Processing Score

In just a few seconds, you receive a score showing how processed the product is, helping you understand its level of transformation.
